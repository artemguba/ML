Ingredients:

1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
4 tbsp butter
4 cloves garlic, minced
1/4 cup white wine (optional)
Juice of 1 lemon
1 tsp lemon zest
1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
Salt and pepper to taste
Red pepper flakes (optional)
Instructions:

In a large skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
Add the shrimp to the skillet and cook until they turn pink, about 2-3 minutes per side.
Pour in the white wine (if using) and lemon juice. Stir in the lemon zest, parsley, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes (if using).
Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, until the sauce has thickened slightly.
Serve the shrimp scampi over pasta or with crusty bread.
