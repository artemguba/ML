For the Dough:

3 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
1 tsp salt
1 tsp instant yeast
1 tbsp sugar
2 tbsp olive oil
1 cup warm water (about 110°F/43°C)
Assembly:

Preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C). If you have a pizza stone, place it in the oven to preheat as well.
Punch down the risen dough and divide it in half (you can freeze the other half for later use).
On a lightly floured surface, roll out the dough to your desired thickness (about 1/4 inch thick is typical for a regular crust).
If using a pizza stone, carefully transfer the rolled-out dough to the preheated stone. If using a baking sheet, lightly grease it and transfer the dough to the sheet.
Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
Sprinkle the shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
Arrange the sliced pepperoni on top of the cheese. Add sliced bell peppers and onions if using.
Sprinkle the grated Parmesan cheese over the toppings.
Drizzle the olive oil over the pizza.
Baking:

Bake the pizza for 15-20 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing.
Garnish with fresh basil leaves if desired.
Serve hot and enjoy!
