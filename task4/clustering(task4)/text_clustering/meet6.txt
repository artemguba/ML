Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the beef and cook until browned on all sides. Remove the beef from the pot and set aside.
In the same pot, add the onion and garlic. Cook until the onion is translucent.
Add the carrots and potatoes, and cook for a few minutes.
Pour in the red wine (if using) and beef broth. Stir in the tomato paste, thyme, rosemary, salt, and pepper.
Return the beef to the pot. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for about 45 minutes, or until the beef is tender.
Stir in the cornstarch mixture to thicken the stew. Cook for an additional 5 minutes.
Serve hot with crusty bread.
