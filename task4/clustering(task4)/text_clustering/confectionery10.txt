Prepare the ingredients. When choosing curd, make sure that it is not too dry (then your curd fritters will be a bit tough), take the one which is rich in fat and moist, but not too much. Knead curd with the fork thoroughly. Add an egg and sugar. Mix everything well.Add flour and mix everything again.Your mixture shouldn't be too dry or too moist. It should be in a form of a lump and keep its shape. Add more flour if needed. Let's start making curd fritters. Take 1 tablespoon of the curd mass with wet hands and form a ball. Flatten it and roll in flour. Place them on a chopping board or a plate. Curd fritters are ready to be fried. 	Put curd fritters on a hot oiled pan. Fry until golden.Adjust the heat to medium. Turn curd fritters over and fry.Curd fritters are ready. Enjoy!
