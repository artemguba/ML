Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper. Place the chicken breasts in a baking dish. Pour the lemon herb mixture over the chicken, making sure each piece is well coated.Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the juices run clear.Let the chicken rest for a few minutes before serving.
