Mix flour, cinnamon, salt and starch in a bowl.	Whip the eggs with sugar for 3-4 minutes with an electric wire whisk. The mixture should lighten a little and increase in volume.	Continuing to whisk, gradually add the flour mixture into the egg mixture. Whisk until it is a homogeneous mass.Peel the apples and cut them into small cubes.	Take a baking dish, flour it lightly and smear with oil. Put the apples in it, and cover with the egg-flour mixture.The apples should be completely covered with the batter. Put in the oven, preheated to 180 degrees, and bake for 30-40 minutes – depending on the features of your oven. As soon as the apple pie is covered with golden crust, check the readiness of the dishes with a toothpick.Let the apple pie cool down and sprinkle it with powdered sugar. The apple pie can be served.
