Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).Place the salmon fillets on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper.Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.While the salmon is baking, prepare the dill sauce. In a small bowl, mix together the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, dill, and garlic.Serve the salmon with the dill sauce on the side.
